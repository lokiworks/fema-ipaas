## Methods
- [Contribute Back](/build-connectors/sharing-connectors/contribute): Publish your connector by contributing back your connector to main repository.
- [Community](/build-connectors/sharing-connectors/community): Publish your connector on npm directly and share it with the community.
- [Private](/build-connectors/sharing-connectors/private): Publish your connector on fema privately.
